INSIGHT 5: Deterministics vs. Sampled Variables
------------------------------------------------
Discovery: The model has two types of variables:
  1. Sampled (free_RVs): intercept, adstock_alpha, gamma_control, etc.
  2. Deterministic: channel_contribution, mu, total_contribution

Pattern: pm.sample() only samples free_RVs, then pm.compute_deterministics()
calculates derived quantities from the samples.

Lesson: This is a performance optimization. Sampling fewer variables is
faster; deterministics can be computed in batch after sampling.

Application: When explaining MCMC sampling, clarify:
  - What gets sampled (parameters with uncertainty)
  - What gets computed (deterministic functions of sampled parameters)
  - Why this distinction matters (performance, interpretability)
